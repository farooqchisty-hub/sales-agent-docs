How it works, end to end

Green steps are the agent. Yellow steps are you.

Import. A list of agency founders comes in as a CSV. Extra columns (country, LinkedIn followers and so on) become facts the agent may use; anything naming another platform is ignored.
Opener. Every agency gets the same approved first email, with their first name and agency filled in. Subject: {agency} x Brevo. It leads with the paid LinkedIn collaboration and asks if they are interested.
Send. It sends in the agency's own morning, 8 to 11am, Tuesday to Friday. Each agency stays on one inbox for the whole thread.
Follow up. No reply means up to 4 follow-ups, 2 days apart. Follow-up 1 carries a ready-to-post LinkedIn announcement in the founder's voice.
Handle replies. Every reply is read, sorted (interested, question, not now, no, wrong person, out of office and so on) and answered politely within working hours. You are CC'd from here.
Book. Interested agencies get your booking link: meet.brevo.com/pushowlpartnerships/new-partners.
Take the call. Open the call brief on Meetings first. Onboard them on PartnerStack.
Close the loop. Create their discount code, email it with the announcement post timeline, and log the outcome in the dashboard.
Everything after a call is booked is yours. The agent never negotiates the collaboration fee, sends a code, or promises anything that is not in the FAQ.

When you step in

  • An alert email (subject starts with Closefast:): someone asked for terms or paperwork, or replied angrily. Open the link in the email.
  • Owe a reply in the summary or the Inbox tab: they wrote and nothing has gone back yet. Under autopilot this should be rare; it means a reply failed. Reply yourself from the thread, or mark No reply needed if it was just a thanks.
  • Draft waiting: a few emails always wait for you, such as the reschedule note after a no-show. Approve, edit or reject them inside the thread.
  • A booking came in: mark it booked (thread or Meetings) and add the time.
If you reply-all to a prospect from your own mailbox (keep the Closefast inbox on the thread), Closefast sees it, marks the thread as yours and stops writing to them. Use Resume automation in the thread to hand it back.

After a call is booked

Before the call. Meetings: open the Call brief. It has who they are, what they replied, the talking point that fits them and the private notes (collaboration fee range, BFCM revenue share, what never to promise).
On the call. Agree the collaboration fee, onboard them on PartnerStack, agree when the founder posts.
Right after. Meetings: click Held, then Won, Nurture or Lost. Add a deal value if you want it in Mission Control.
Follow-through. Create their discount code and email it with the post timeline. This part is manual.
No-show. Click No-show. A reschedule note is drafted for you to approve in the thread or Approvals.

Guardrails (hard, even for you)

  • No money amounts in writing. A draft with a fee or amount cannot be sent, even if you approve it. Amounts are for the call.
  • Blocked topics: acquisition, merger, roll-up, exit, valuation, long-term plans, lead flow, "sell your agency". Same rule.
  • No other platforms: Klaviyo, Omnisend, Mailchimp, Attentive and Postscript are blocked. We only talk about Brevo.
  • Never says: guaranteed leads, we will send you clients, exclusive, signing bonus.
  • Polite always. Short sentences, no hype, one ask, no pressure.
  • No double sends. One email per step per person, ever. Opt-outs are permanent.

How it's built (one minute)

  • Runs on Cloudflare: a Worker wakes every 5 minutes, checks the inboxes, drafts, sends what is due and writes the summaries. Data lives in a D1 database. Writing uses Workers AI models.
  • Sending: 4 Instantly inboxes used by no other engine. Each starts at 10 emails a day and adds 5 per sending day. One inbox per agency for the whole thread.
  • Founders first: where we have several contacts at one agency, the founder is emailed first; the others only if the founder stays quiet, and all stop when anyone replies.
  • The brief: everything the agent writes comes from the campaign brief (offer, talking points, FAQ, guardrails). Change the brief, and every new email changes.
  • Learning: your edits, rejections and Teach notes become rules on the campaign.
  • Same family as the creator seeding and Partner Voices engines, rebuilt for booking calls with agency owners.

FAQ

Will the agent ever send the discount code or agree a fee?

No. It cannot write amounts, and codes are created by you after the call.

Someone booked through the link but Closefast still shows "interested".

Booking detection from your calendar is not connected yet. Mark it booked in the thread or on Meetings. That also writes the call brief and pauses their colleagues.

I replied from Gmail. Will the agent reply on top of me?

No, as long as you reply-all so the Closefast inbox is on it. Your reply marks the thread as yours and the agent stops. If you emailed them separately, click Take over in the thread.

Why has nobody been emailed at 4pm?

Cold emails only go out 8 to 11am in the agency's own timezone, Tuesday to Friday. Replies go out 7am to 9pm their time, Monday to Saturday.

A thread looks wrong or someone is upset.

Take over the thread, or press Emergency stop if it looks systemic, then tell Farooq.

How do I add the next batch of agencies?

Campaigns, open the campaign, Import a CSV (name, email, company, website, role, country, plus any extra columns). Farooq can do this for you.
