0. The thesis

The agent loop (research, write, send, follow up, handle replies, book) is commodity code, so charging seats for it is SaaS 1.0. We host it and give it away forever, Semrush-style: always something free, always limited. Free sends from our own warm inboxes, so there's nothing to connect, no risk to the user's domain, and the first replies arrive within days. We charge for the fuel: more sending, sending from their own brand, more found prospects, more signals and better writing. One prepaid wallet, priced per use, no seats, no outcome guarantees.

Two layers of free do two jobs. The starter boost (once, at signup) gets a new user to real replies fast. The always-free allowance (every month, no expiry) keeps them coming back weekly and keeps them in our database, at under $3 a month even when maxed out.

Hosting everything means we own the relationship: every signup, their ideal customer, usage and results live with us. Users who keep hitting the caps are upgrade candidates we can see, and Closefast itself can run our outreach to them.

1. What the market is telling us

Greg Isenberg, "SaaS is dead?"

  • The tool is now easy to build, so give it away.
  • Free users pay in data, which builds network effects and a moat.
  • Upsell intelligence (agents, billed per use) and human service (a monthly fee).
  • SMBs trend to free; enterprises still pay for security and reassurance.

Explee (ex AutoGTM)

  • Hosted, no subscription. Lead finding free, about $0.03 to $0.04 per email, $30 trial credit (card required).
  • Sends from its own pool of pre-warmed inboxes: zero setup, which is why its trial converts.
  • Claims 76,371 hot leads for 7,171 companies; its own estimate: 750 emails bring 2 to 6 warm leads and about 1 meeting.
  • Weak spots in reviews: no own domain or sender names, sends before leads are approved.

Jungler

  • $99 to $299 a month for data only: who engaged with competitor, industry and creator posts, plus authors of keyword posts.
  • Bills every raw like or comment, even people your filter drops. Never sends anything.

Closefast today

  • Approval-first agent with reply handling, booking, guardrails and duplicate-send protection, live on the Brevo pilot: 50 prospects, 2 replies so far (4%).
  • Every table already carries a workspace id, so going multi-tenant is an extension, not a rewrite.
  • This model replaces the $49 license offer.

2. Vocabulary: prospect, lead, goal

The market uses "lead" for three different things: any uploaded contact (Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist), someone who engaged with a post (Jungler), or someone who showed interest (HubSpot). We pick one meaning and keep it everywhere: a lead is earned by a reply. Everyone before that is a prospect.

4. The free plan: never expires

Starter boost (once, first 14 days)

  • Paste your website: the agent works out your buyers and finds 100 prospects (company, decision maker, verified email) from whichever sources fit your business.
  • Up to 300 emails from our warm inboxes (about 75 prospects with follow-ups), 25 a day.
  • If your buyers are on LinkedIn, an opt-in signal backfilled over the last 30 days: people engaging with a competitor's posts or posts on a keyword. Its matches count toward the 100.
  • Replies handled, booking link sent, every thread in one inbox.
  • Our cost when fully used: about $6.80, once.

Always free (every month, forever)

  • 50 found prospects a month, from any source that fits.
  • 5 emails a day from our warm inboxes (about 110 a month, about 28 prospects).
  • The opt-in signal keeps running weekly; its matches come out of the 50 and arrive as a weekly "your buyers this week" digest.
  • Unlimited reply handling on their threads.
  • Our cost when maxed out: about $2.70 a month.

Why these caps: found prospects are cheap (about $0.008 each from data, about the same from a signal), so free gets plenty. Sending is the real cost (about $0.018 an email from our pre-warmed pool, about 4 emails per prospect), so that's what we cap. The weekly signal digest is the cheapest way to give a reason to come back every week.

6. Sending: our pool for free, their brand for paid

Free: our warm inbox pool

  • Pre-warmed Google domains and inboxes bought from InboxKit's inventory (they come with neutral names), with warming kept on.
  • Sender name is the user's real name; the signature, footer, address and unsubscribe are their business's. Replies land in their Closefast inbox and are forwarded to their email.
  • Each user is pinned to 1 or 2 inboxes, so one bad sender only burns their own slice.
  • Cost: about $6 to $9 per inbox a month plus a one-time domain fee, for about 550 safe emails: about $0.018 an email with a 30% burn reserve. Growing the pool later with fresh inboxes we warm ahead of demand brings it back toward $0.01.

Paid: their brand

  • Lookalike branded domains (acme.com becomes getacme.com), registered on our Cloudflare account, with inboxes created, authenticated and warmed by the InboxKit API.
  • Or connect their own Gmail or Outlook, unlimited.
  • "Send as your own brand" plus higher volume is a natural upgrade trigger.

9. Signals (the Jungler module): a source, not a plan

  • Only where buyers are on LinkedIn. Signals fit B2B: SaaS, agencies, B2B services, recruiting. A real estate agent's buyers or a dentist's patients aren't engaging with competitor posts on LinkedIn; their sources are local directories and maps, property or public data, and their own lists. The agent reads the website at signup, works out who the buyers are, and only offers signals when they fit. This is the Source Plan idea Closefast already uses.
  • Opt-in, never automatic. If they fit, onboarding shows one card: "Your buyers are active on LinkedIn. Watch who engages with a competitor's posts?" The user picks 1 competitor page or keyword. Free matches come out of the same prospect allowance, so nobody pays for a source they don't use.
  • Paid: metered from the wallet, $0.15 per signal prospect (people who match only), with more signals and daily refresh. Not a separate plan: Valley bundles signals into a $199+ seat and Jungler sells them at $99+ a month, while ours costs nothing until it finds a match. A monthly "signals pack" can come later if customers want a fixed bill.
  • Copy rule: a signal is a reason to write, never the subject. Never say "I saw you liked a post" and never name the competitor.

10. Model choice

GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 cost the same on OpenRouter: $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output, and half that in batch mode. So the pick comes down to quality: run both on a reply-rate eval and keep the winner, with OpenRouter making a swap a config change. The real saving is batching research and draft writing (they're not urgent), which halves those calls. With that, a fully worked prospect costs about $0.003 in LLM.

11. Economics

Free

  • About $6.80 once per fully used boost, then at most $2.70 a month.
  • Per 1,000 signups: about $2,700 once plus about $240 a month.
  • If 3% of signups pay $75 a month, that's about $1,700 a month of gross profit (75% margin): it repays the boosts in under 2 months and covers the always-free cost about 7 times over.

Paid: 500 prospects a month (2,000 emails), all found by us, 4 branded inboxes on 2 domains

  • They pay: prospects worked $15 + prospects found $40 + inboxes $20 + domains $2.50 = $77.50 a month. Explee for the same 2,000 emails: $70 to $80, from Explee's domains.
  • We spend: about $19.70. Gross margin about 75%.
  • Add 200 signal matches: +$30 for about $2 of data.

12. What hosting adds to the build

  • Multi-tenant: signup, login, workspaces, per-tenant allowances and a usage ledger.
  • Warm pool manager: buys pre-warmed inboxes from InboxKit ahead of demand, pins users to inboxes, tracks bounces and complaints per user, retires burned inboxes.
  • Branded inboxes: InboxKit API plus Cloudflare Registrar for the paid tier.
  • Connect your own inbox (paid): Google requires app verification plus an annual security assessment for apps that read Gmail, and Microsoft asks for publisher verification. Free no longer depends on it, so it can come later.
  • Billing: Stripe wallet, top-ups, auto top-up, bounce refunds, live usage.
  • Pool protection, invisible to the user: daily caps, verified emails only, content gates, auto-pause on bounces or complaints, cost per account in the admin console. No signup friction.
