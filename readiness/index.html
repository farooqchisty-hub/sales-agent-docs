What changed for customers

  • Colleagues in a prospect's thread. A colleague's "remove me" removes only the colleague: the prospect stays in outreach, is not tagged opted out, and the reply counts leave it out. Replies to a colleague name who receives them (Approvals, thread, summary), are checked against that person (no false greeting warning), and the Reply box can write to the prospect or any colleague who wrote, nobody else. If our answer to a colleague bounces, only the colleague is suppressed: the prospect is never closed or refunded by it, and nothing is shown as owed to someone we can't reach. An email the owner links by hand is the prospect's own.
  • Sending. An approved reply goes out the same day even when cold emails used the inbox's daily limit (replies stop at a higher safety ceiling, and both share one counter, so two passes can never overshoot). Every approve says truthfully when the email will go: the window, the daily limit, test mode, a paused or removed inbox, credits. What's next in a thread says the same, and promises follow-ups only when one is coming. The test-mode-off dialog counts what the day's limit and spacing really allow.
  • Guidance. When an inbox is removed and its campaign pauses, Home, the thread, the notice (with an Open button) and the summary email all say so and where to fix it. Home shows failed email checks whenever there are any. Every rate on Results names what it is a share of. The summary, Home and Results count replies the same way, by person.
  • Hand back after Take over writes a fresh reply for each person still owed one, once, however many clicks or tabs.
  • Plain words. No raw error text reaches a customer (inbox errors, web research, previews, Signals, imports and 20 more places), intents read as words ("asked to be removed", not "unsubscribe"), plurals are right, and a person with no name shows by email everywhere.
  • Phones and pages. A Not matched email opens on a phone so it can be linked. The email confirm and unsubscribe pages follow dark mode. A second confirm click logs nothing more. The Inbox list updates as soon as you take over or hand back.
  • Operator console. Its requests carry their real Origin in every browser (Firefox and Safari would have been refused), and its confirm dialog keeps keyboard focus inside.

Many users at once

Goal: when many customers use Closefast at the same time, none of them can slow down, starve or break another. None can see or change another's data. Races can never cause a double send, a double charge, a lost reply or a stuck state. Any failure stays inside the account it belongs to.

Fair schedulingEach account gets its own cron invocation through a queue, so its own 1,000 D1 queries and CPU time (Cloudflare limits checked 19 Nov). Without the queue, accounts run in fair rotation with their own time and query budget.
Race safetyAtomic locks and claims for overlapping passes. Idempotent sends and charges. Double clicks, two teammates at once, and the cron racing pause, approve, remove inbox, import and archive.
Failure containmentTimeouts on every mail and vendor call. One account's silent mail server, failing vendor or huge import cannot hold up anyone else.
Proof30 simulated accounts under load for at least 10 minutes, with overlapping cron passes and injected faults: hangs, greylisting, rejects, dropped connections and crashes mid-send. An invariant checker must pass every rule.

Invariants the battle test enforces

  • No step of a sequence is ever sent twice, and the outbox matches the sent records one to one.
  • Nothing goes to paused, stopped, suppressed, archived or opted-out people.
  • Every message's inbox belongs to the same account as its prospect.
  • Wallet balance equals the ledger, with no duplicate charge or refund.
  • Nothing stays stuck in 'sending' after a crash.
  • Every active account makes progress within a bounded number of passes.
  • Replies land in the right account and conversation.
  • Shared warm inbox caps are never exceeded across accounts.
  • Core pages stay fast under load.

Positioning

Closefast is the AI sales agent for small B2B teams that want booked conversations without hiring an SDR or learning five tools. Paste your website and it works out who buys, finds them three ways (people search, your own list, or people engaging with LinkedIn posts about your problem right now), writes short honest emails from your inbox or our warm ones, follows up, answers replies and hands you the interested ones. Nothing goes out until you start people, every claim comes from facts you approved, and you pay only for prospects delivered and emails sent, with your own list from your own inbox free. Versus Instantly or lemlist you do not build sequences; versus 11x or Artisan you do not sign an annual contract.

  • Terms and Privacy pages do not exist yet, and the signup should link to them.
  • The monthly data ceiling is max($100, $50 per paying account). Confirm or change it.
  • Switch on the per-account cron queue before about 30 active accounts (one Cloudflare Queue and two config blocks).
  • Not exercised live: real Signals and people-search buys end to end, model-written replies in the walkthroughs (no model locally), follow-ups over real days, and account mode.
  • Also not exercised locally: a web research error shown on the campaign page (it would have needed outside network calls), and delivery-report bounces from Gmail, Microsoft or Instantly for a colleague (send-time refusals were tested).
