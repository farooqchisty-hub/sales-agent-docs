Verdict

Not production ready today. The audit found 10 P0 issues: things that send email people asked us not to send, send the same email twice, charge without delivering, or let one account stall everyone. None of them is hard to fix. All are being fixed now, together with 34 P1 and 58 P2 issues, before anything ships.

Release gate: every P0 and P1 fixed and reviewed. The full test suite green. The multi-tenant battle test passing every invariant under load and injected failures. A new-customer browser walkthrough clean at desktop and phone width. A production smoke check after deploy.

Fix packages

Each package is built in its own branch by one engineer agent, then reviewed by an independent adversarial reviewer, fixed, and reviewed again until it passes. Then the packages are merged one at a time, with the full test suite run after each merge.

WP1 Platform: cron isolation, mail I/O, inbox lifecycle, pool and moneyMake the cron and the mail layer bounded and fail-safe. Each workspace gets its own time and query budget. Every network wait gets a timeout. Removing or pausing an inbox never loses a reply, and stop/resume no longer rewrites the queue. Instantly bounces feed back into Closefast. Hot tables get indexes. Failing stages raise incidents and operator alerts, and incidents clear when fixed. Erasure removes the data.

Honour every way of saying stop (mailto unsubscribe, 'no'). Surface warm replies and refund bounced prospects whatever the source. Keep the agent from booking times without a link. Thread correctly, space cold sends, fix the writing gates for meeting-adjacent sellers, and finish the small stop-rule gaps.

Many users at once

Goal: when many customers use Closefast at the same time, none of them can slow down, starve or break another. None can see or change another's data. Races can never cause a double send, a double charge, a lost reply or a stuck state. Any failure stays inside the account it belongs to.

Fair schedulingEach account gets its own cron invocation through a queue, so its own 1,000 D1 queries and CPU time (Cloudflare limits checked 19 Nov). Without the queue, accounts run in fair rotation with their own time and query budget.
Race safetyAtomic locks and claims for overlapping passes. Idempotent sends and charges. Double clicks, two teammates at once, and the cron racing pause, approve, remove inbox, import and archive.
Failure containmentTimeouts on every mail and vendor call. One account's silent mail server, failing vendor or huge import cannot hold up anyone else.
Proof30 simulated accounts under load for at least 10 minutes, with overlapping cron passes and injected faults: hangs, greylisting, rejects, dropped connections and crashes mid-send. An invariant checker must pass every rule.

Invariants the battle test enforces

  • No step of a sequence is ever sent twice, and the outbox matches the sent records one to one.
  • Nothing goes to paused, stopped, suppressed, archived or opted-out people.
  • Every message's inbox belongs to the same account as its prospect.
  • Wallet balance equals the ledger, with no duplicate charge or refund.
  • Nothing stays stuck in 'sending' after a crash.
  • Every active account makes progress within a bounded number of passes.
  • Replies land in the right account and conversation.
  • Shared warm inbox caps are never exceeded across accounts.
  • Core pages stay fast under load.

Positioning

Closefast is the AI sales agent for small B2B teams that want booked conversations without hiring an SDR or learning five tools. Paste your website and it works out who buys, finds them three ways (people search, your own list, or people engaging with LinkedIn posts about your problem right now), writes short honest emails from your inbox or our warm ones, follows up, answers replies and hands you the interested ones. Nothing goes out until you start people, every claim comes from facts you approved, and you pay only for prospects delivered and emails sent, with your own list from your own inbox free. Versus Instantly or lemlist you do not build sequences; versus 11x or Artisan you do not sign an annual contract.

Owner actions and what was not covered

  • Hosted Google and Microsoft inbox sign-in needs Closefast-owned, verified Google and Microsoft apps. Until then, company inboxes connect with an app password, and the new guided connect fills in the server details.
  • The per-account cron queue needs one Cloudflare Queue created at deploy. Without it, the fair rotation fallback runs.
  • The Instantly bounce field is coded defensively and will be confirmed against the one known bounced lead after deploy.
  • Dodo refund and dispute payloads were not seen live, so they are handled defensively.
  • Not exercised live: real Signals buys, real people-search buys, follow-ups over real days, and account mode.
  • 24 P3 polish items are in the backlog. The cheap and safe ones are included in the packages.
