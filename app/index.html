Closefast: the app

The screens a buyer lives in after install. Built around one question a founder asks every morning: did it book calls, and does it need me? Everything creator-specific (fees, followers, payouts, negotiation rounds) is gone; deals, meetings, objections, deliverability and pipeline value replace it. Sample data uses the landing page's running example, Brightline Studio.

Three panes: filter by intent, the thread, and the lead's context. One glance shows why the agent said what it said.
Intent labels replace creator stages: interested, question, objection, not now, no, out of office, unsubscribe.
Every draft shows its gates (fact-check, ceiling, salutation, banned words) before you approve.

3. Approvals

A stack, not a table. One draft at a time, keyboard shortcuts (A approve, E edit, R reject), built to clear 20 in five minutes.
Citations visible. Each highlighted claim links to the page it came from, so trust is earned fast.
Reject teaches. A rejection reason becomes a learned rule for that campaign, shown back as "rule added".

4. Meetings

The new sales-only screen. The agent's job ends at a booked call, but the buyer's job starts there.
Prep brief per call: who they are, why they fit, what they said, the objection to expect, a suggested opener.
Outcome logging (held, no-show, interested, deal value) closes the loop so segments are judged on deals, not replies.

Brief and rules keeps versions, so "what changed before replies dropped?" has an answer.
